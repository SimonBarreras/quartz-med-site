# Apoptosis Quiz: Study Notes

Oct 16, 2026 · @simon

The 20 questions from the Aula Virtual apoptosis quiz. The platform had not released the official answers yet, so the answers below are worked out from standard cell biology. Check them against the official key once grades are published.

## Multiple choice and true/false

**1. What happens to DNA during apoptosis that can be observed as a ladder pattern on gels?** A) Internucleosomal DNA fragmentation · B) DNA repair synthesis without breaks · C) Circularization of DNA molecules · D) Random DNA giant duplications · E) Complete DNA integrity without fragmentation **Answer: A.** Caspase-activated DNase cuts between nucleosomes, giving fragments in multiples of about 180-200 bp.

**2. Alzheimer's involves neuronal death by apoptosis; inhibiting which protein could prevent progression?** A) Bcl-2 · B) Bax · C) Cyclin-D · D) Bcl-XL **Answer: B (Bax).** It is the only proapoptotic option; Bcl-2 and Bcl-XL are antiapoptotic.

**3. p53 activates...** A) proapoptotic proteins Bax and Bad · B) Bax and Bcl-xL · C) Bcl-2 and Bcl-xL · D) Bcl-2 and Bax **Answer: A.** The only option where both proteins are proapoptotic. (Classic direct p53 targets are Bax, PUMA and Noxa.)

**5. True/False: Programmed cell death (apoptosis) results in a powerful inflammation process.** **Answer: False.** Apoptotic cells are packaged into apoptotic bodies and phagocytosed without inflammation; necrosis is what causes inflammation.

**6. Which pathway is primarily activated by Fas ligand binding to Fas receptor?** A) Mitochondrial biogenesis · B) Necrotic · C) Autophagic · D) Intrinsic only · E) Extrinsic **Answer: E (extrinsic).** Fas is a death receptor; it recruits FADD and activates caspase-8.

**7. What role does p53 play in the cellular response to DNA damage?** A) Inhibits all apoptotic pathways · B) Directly degrades damaged DNA · C) Always promotes cell division · D) Only functions in the cytoplasm · E) Can halt the cell cycle or promote apoptosis if damage is irreparable **Answer: E.** p53 induces p21 (G1 arrest) to allow repair, and triggers apoptosis if repair fails.

**8. Choose the right answer: "Bcl-2..."** A) opens pores in the mitochondria · B) is a proapoptotic factor · C) binds to cytochrome c to inhibit apoptosis · D) captures Bax to prevent apoptosis **Answer: D.** Bcl-2 is antiapoptotic and sequesters Bax/Bak so they cannot permeabilize the mitochondrial outer membrane.

**9. In cancer, resistance to chemotherapy can arise from failure to activate which pathway?** A) Mitochondrial biogenesis · B) Proapoptotic pathways (Bax/Bak and caspase activation) · C) Autophagy exclusively · D) Necrosis · E) Necroptosis **Answer: B.** Many chemotherapy drugs kill by inducing apoptosis, so a blocked apoptotic pathway means resistance.

**10. Which protein is commonly overexpressed as an antiapoptotic factor and can sequester proapoptotic proteins?** A) Bad · B) Bid · C) Caspase-3 · D) p53 · E) Bcl-2 **Answer: E (Bcl-2).** Overexpressed in follicular lymphoma via t(14;18).

**11. Which of the following is a proapoptotic member of the Bcl-2 family?** A) Bcl-2 · B) Mdm2 · C) p53 · D) Bax · E) Bcl-xL **Answer: D (Bax).** Mdm2 and p53 are not Bcl-2 family members.

**12. Indicate the false answer:** A) Phosphatidylserine translocates during apoptosis from the outer side of the membrane to the inner side · B) DNA is fragmented into high and low molecular weight units · C) Membrane blebs are typical of apoptosis but not necrosis · D) Bcl-2 is involved in the intrinsic pathway **Answer: A (false).** The direction is reversed: PS moves from the inner leaflet to the outer leaflet.

**13. True/False: Death receptors are associated with the extrinsic pathway of apoptosis.** **Answer: True.**

**15. What is the consequence of phosphatidylserine translocation during early apoptosis?** A) Activation of autophagy · B) Externalized PS signals phagocytes for engulfment · C) Stabilization of membrane integrity · D) Activation of necroptosis · E) Inhibition of DNA fragmentation **Answer: B.** Exposed PS is an "eat me" signal (also what Annexin V staining detects).

**17. Which best differentiates apoptosis from necrosis?** A) Apoptosis results only from external injury · B) Apoptosis is programmed and energy-dependent with minimal inflammation · C) Necrosis involves mitochondrial pore formation and caspase activation · D) Apoptosis always causes acute inflammation · E) Necrosis is programmed and energy-dependent with no inflammation **Answer: B.**

**18. Choose the false answer regarding Apaf-1:** A) Part of the intrinsic pathway · B) Forms part of the apoptosome when it binds cytochrome c · C) It is formed after the activation of Fas · D) It is formed as a result of DNA damage that cannot be repaired **Answer: C (false).** Fas activates the extrinsic pathway (caspase-8); Apaf-1 belongs to the intrinsic pathway.

**19. The activation of procaspases is fundamentally mediated by:** A) Phosphorylation · B) Oligomerization · C) Proteolytic cleavage · D) Ubiquitination **Answer: C (proteolytic cleavage).** Caveat: initiator caspases (8, 9) are activated by induced proximity/dimerization in the DISC or apoptosome, so if your lecturer stressed that, B could be the intended answer. Effector caspases (3, 7) are activated by cleavage.

**20. What is the role of Apaf-1 in apoptosis?** A) Forms the apoptosome that activates caspase-9 · B) Restarts DNA replication · C) Inhibits caspase activation · D) Binds Fas ligand · E) Degrades cytochrome c **Answer: A.**

## Sentence questions ("oración confusa")

These were sentence-building questions; the correct sentences were:

**4.** The intrinsic pathway is primarily activated by DNA damage and cellular stress.

**14.** The extrinsic pathway of apoptosis is initiated by Fas binding to its receptor. (Strictly, Fas ligand binds the Fas receptor.)

**16.** Activation of p53 leads to upregulation of proapoptotic Bax, promoting mitochondrial outer membrane permeabilization.

## Key facts to remember

- **Antiapoptotic:** Bcl-2, Bcl-xL. **Proapoptotic:** Bax, Bak, Bad, Bid, PUMA, Noxa.
- **Intrinsic pathway:** DNA damage/stress → p53 → Bax/Bak → cytochrome c release → Apaf-1 + cytochrome c = apoptosome → caspase-9 → caspase-3.
- **Extrinsic pathway:** FasL binds Fas (death receptor) → FADD → caspase-8 → caspase-3.
- **Apoptosis hallmarks:** energy-dependent, no inflammation, membrane blebbing, PS externalized, DNA ladder.
- **Necrosis:** passive, cell swelling and lysis, inflammation.
